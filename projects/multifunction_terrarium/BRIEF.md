# Terrarium — brief

Nothing is designed yet. This file exists so the requirements stay put and we stop
circling.

## Confirmed requirements (from the original ask, unchanged)

- Multichamber terrarium with **multichamber water levels**, water flowing through
- **Watering pipes**
- **Thin wall, mostly viewing ports**
- **Organic tubes running vertically** through it
- **Trays with domes that snap-fit on and lift off** to clean and prune
- Occult / hermetic features
- Permaculture function stacking
- Purpose: demonstrate **vibration and correspondence** at a small living scale
- **Base no bigger than 320 × 320 mm** (printer bed is 320³, PETG)

## Settled

- Livestock: moss, springtails, isopods, worms, snails. **No fish** (a 320 footprint
  can't hold enough water for one without wrecking the form).
- Solar only. That runs a pump (~0.5 W) but cannot run a heater (~25 W).

## Rejected — do not propose again

1. **Smooth waisted ovoid**, three lobes, glass-marble lenses in a continuous skin.
   Wrong: dropped the tray/dome requirement, converted "mostly viewing ports" into a
   solid skin, buried the vertical tubes inside the wall.
2. **Vertically stacked intersecting spheres.**
3. **Tripod-of-Life bubble cluster** with a Flower-of-Life lattice shell and trefoil
   lift-off cap — wide and low, 317 × 185.

## Worth keeping from the discarded work

These were sound and are reusable whatever the form turns out to be:

- A **bell siphon is a genuine relaxation oscillator** — no timer, no electronics. It is
  the honest way to demonstrate vibration.
- **Three siphons with flood volumes at 1:2:3** beat against each other on a common
  period of 6T. Three oscillators, one law, harmonic ratio.
- Nesting the same accumulate → threshold → discharge → reset law across drip (~0.2 s),
  siphon (~10 min) and solar day (24 h) spans 5.5 decades and is measurable. That is
  correspondence you can put a stopwatch on, rather than assert.
- A worm/litter bed over water must be a **trickle** bed with an air gap beneath and a
  ≥30° drain slope. If it ponds it goes anaerobic and poisons everything below.
- Hermetic principles should map onto **working parts**, never be engraved on.

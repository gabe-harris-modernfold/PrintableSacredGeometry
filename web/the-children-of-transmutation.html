A catalogue and a chronicle

The Children of Transmutation

What humans have turned into what — energy, matter and the self — and how to tell which kind of turning each one was.

“I am a child of Earth and starry Heaven.”Orphic gold tablet, Petelia, 4th century BC: the password of a soul seeking release

Nine-tenths of the mass of your body was made by transmutation. The hydrogen came out of the first minutes of the universe; nearly everything else, the oxygen in your blood, the carbon in every cell, the calcium of your bones, was fused inside stars and scattered when they died. You are a child of transmutation in the most literal sense physics allows.

For about a million years the children have been transmuting in return. They have turned wood into warmth, wind into motion, falling water into light and, since 1919, one element into another. For as long, they have practised turning themselves into something else: essence into spirit, poison into wisdom, the lead of the soul into its gold.

This page catalogues both and keeps them apart. A motor, a nuclear reaction and Daoist inner alchemy do not make the same kind of claim, so every entry carries a chip that says which kind it makes.

IBorn of transmutation

Weigh a body by where its atoms were made. About a tenth of its mass is hydrogen, which came out of the first minutes of the universe. Nearly all the rest was fused inside stars: the oxygen and much of the carbon in massive stars that later exploded, the rest of the carbon and most of the nitrogen in smaller stars that shed their outer layers as they died. Traces came from stranger furnaces: in 2017 telescopes watched two neutron stars collide and make heavy elements like the iodine in your thyroid, of which you carry about ten milligrams.

    Body composition by mass after ICRP Reference Man and Emsley; stellar origins after Johnson (2019). How the carbon divides between kinds of star is an estimate; the trace slice is drawn wider than its true share so it can be seen.

    IIThree kinds of change, and the Art between them

    There is no finite, agreed list of “all energetic transmutations”. The phrase covers three subjects that answer three different questions, and a fourth that sat between them for two thousand years.

    Energy changes form

    Wood into warmth, falling water into current. Energy is conserved: never made or destroyed, only moved from one form to another, and every step sheds some as heat. A conversion is done when a device does it on demand.

    What forms of energy have humans converted? A catalogue of mechanisms and devices.

    Matter changes element

    An element is fixed by the number of protons in its nucleus. Change that number and the element changes; change only the neutrons and you have a new isotope of the same element. Done by bombardment, neutron capture, fission and fusion, never by ordinary chemistry.

    What matter have humans transmuted? A record of nuclear reactions.

    The Art

    Laboratory alchemy worked on matter with furnaces and stills and on the soul with images, often in the same book. Its claims to change one metal into another were tried for fifteen centuries and never shown; its methods, apparatus and words became chemistry.

    Where the other three met, and were later pulled apart.

    The self changes state

    Essence into spirit, the commanding self into the self at peace, poison into wisdom. Here “performed” can mean that people practised a discipline, experienced a change, or reported reaching a goal. Jing, qi and shen, the nafs and kundalini are categories of cultivation, not other names for joules.

    What inner energies have humans transmuted? A catalogue of practices, experiences and claims across traditions.

      IVThe octagon of forms

      Eight forms of energy, and every conversion between them that people have harnessed or built. An arrow marks the principal useful conversion; real devices make several outputs at once, waste heat among them. A dashed arrow is a chain rather than a single step, and it is drawn running through the form it passes through: an engine turns fuel into motion only by way of heat, while a muscle does it directly.

      The difference matters. A fuel-burning power station does not turn chemical energy into electricity; it goes through heat and motion first. A photovoltaic cell turns light into current with no heat-engine stage, and a thermoelectric junction turns a temperature difference into current with no rotating generator.

      Touch a form, or an arrow. Gold arrows leave the chosen form; blue arrows arrive.

      Eight is a grouping, not a law: physics counts forms differently for different purposes. Sound is mechanical energy travelling through matter, kept apart here because we hear it as its own thing.

      VThe inheritance of a joule

      Follow one hundred joules from a source to a lamp. Each conversion is a child that keeps only part of what it inherits; the rest leaves as heat. Faint stages are the ancestry, where the numbers are too uncertain to draw. Change the lamp and see how much the last step decides.

      VIThe ladder of elements

      Proton number climbs the page; time runs across it. Arrows are transmutations people made or first understood. Dots are the elements people made that nature had lost or never made in quantity. Most transmutations move one or two rungs. Fission is the long fall.

      In 2025 CERN's ALICE collaboration reported that when lead nuclei narrowly miss each other in the LHC, the intense electromagnetic field can knock three protons out of one, turning lead (82 protons) into gold (79). The gold nuclei are so energetic that they break apart almost at once on striking the accelerator. It is real transmutation, not bullion. Mercury can be made into gold too, first in 1941, but never as an economical way to make gold.

      Below iron (26), fusing nuclei releases energy; above it, splitting them does, which is why stars stop at iron and why fission falls toward it. Above uranium (92) nothing survives in nature in quantity. A ring marks a one-rung change too short to draw as an arrow; gold marks every arrow that ends at gold.

      VIILadders of refinement

      Twelve traditions' maps of inner transmutation, each drawn from the dense at the bottom to the subtle at the top and stretched to the same height, so their shapes can be compared. They are not one ladder. Some are sequences, some are pairs (a poison and the wisdom it becomes, dashed), and every rung is the tradition's own category, not a quantity. Choose a rung.

      Where instruments have looked

      A few practices have had their bodily effects measured. The measurement says what changed in the body; it does not say what the tradition says changed.

      VIIIWhere the lanes touch

      Some links between the lanes are history: one grew out of the other. A few are measurements: an instrument from one lane pointed at a practice from another. Most are rhymes, the same shape at two scales, and one is a contrast. Each card says which, what recurs, and what the change of scale alters.

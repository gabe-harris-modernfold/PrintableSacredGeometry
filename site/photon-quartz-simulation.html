SOURCE — two declared modes, toggled in the controls. Sunlight (default): an extended, incoherent, unpolarized disk of angular diameter 0.533° at infinity, broadband, its direct-beam power computed through the atmosphere you set below (≈ 0.9 kW/m² on a clear day) — the lens images it to a disk Ø f·θ☉, and single-λ readouts evaluate its 589 nm component. Laboratory: a monochromatic point-like emitter at λ = 589 nm (ν ≈ 508.99 THz, hν ≈ 2.105 eV), waist w₀ = 50 µm — the coherent idealization whose Gaussian focusing Self's equation drives.

QUARTZ — a positive uniaxial crystal, n₀ = 1.5443, nₑ = 1.5534 at 589 nm (dispersion not modeled); the optic axis lies at angle θ in the drawing plane (hatch marks in the block). Media change λ and phase velocity, never ν. Birefringence vanishes at θ = 0°.

MARKERS & OBSERVER — the animated ticks are wave-front phase markers moving at c/n, not photons; the observer is the Navarro schematic eye (four refracting surfaces, dispersive media, a crystalline lens that reshapes as it accommodates; axial 23.92 mm), accommodating within the amplitude its age allows, pupil Ø 4 mm at L = 250 mm.

THE FULL JOURNEY — below the bench, the same model is followed across the whole solar spectrum: atmosphere (sun elevation, temperature, pressure, humidity, haze), the magnifier glass, the crystal's surfaces, colour centres, lattice phonons and heat, and the eye's tear film, cornea, lens, vitreous, macular pigment and cone pigments, ending in a ledger of everything that happens but is never seen.
